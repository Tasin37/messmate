# Glossary

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Term                                Meaning
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Mess                                A shared household/accommodation
                                      group; tenant boundary

  Member                              A person with an active or
                                      historical membership in a mess

  Manager                             A member with administrative
                                      permissions

  Manager rotation                    Schedule that assigns daily/monthly
                                      operational duty

  Meal slot                           Breakfast, lunch or dinner

  Meal unit                           Billable quantity for a member and
                                      meal slot

  Guest meal                          Meal consumed by a visitor and
                                      attributed according to mess policy

  Meal pool                           Expenses used to calculate meal
                                      rate

  Meal rate                           Meal-pool expense divided by
                                      billable meal units

  Shared bill                         Rent, utilities or other non-meal
                                      shared charge

  Expense credit                      Credit for a member who paid an
                                      approved mess expense

  Deposit                             Money paid by a member to the mess

  Due                                 Amount a member owes the mess

  Advance                             Amount the mess owes/holds for a
                                      member

  Settlement                          Final calculation of a member's
                                      charges, credits and payments

  Accounting period                   A calendar month used for mess
                                      accounting

  Month close                         Process that freezes a reconciled
                                      period

  Audit log                           Append-oriented record of who
                                      changed what and when

  Tenant isolation                    Preventing one mess from accessing
                                      another mess's data
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
