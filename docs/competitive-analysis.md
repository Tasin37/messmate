# Competitive Analysis and Positioning

## 1. Scope and caveat

This is a product strategy hypothesis, not a verified live-market
survey. Validate competitor feature availability, pricing, regional
adoption and current reviews before making public comparison claims.

## 3. Differentiation hypotheses

-   Bangladesh-focused meal-rate and mess settlement model.
-   Bangla-first UX and BDT defaults.
-   Clear separation between bazaar expense credits and member deposits.
-   Manager rotation schedule for daily or monthly duty.
-   Audit trail and month locking to reduce disputes.
-   Mobile-first member self-service and invite-based onboarding.

## 4. Positioning statement

"For bachelor messes that need a fair and transparent monthly হিসাব,
Mess Manager combines meal tracking, bazaar expenses, shared bills and
member deposits in one simple mobile-friendly workspace."

## 5. Validation plan

Interview 8--12 mess managers and 15--20 members. Ask: - How do they
calculate meal rate today? - Which expenses are included in meal rate? -
How are rent and utilities divided? - How are guests, absences and late
meal-off requests handled? - What causes the most disputes? - Who is
allowed to edit records? - Would they pay, and which pricing model feels
acceptable?

Observe one real month-end calculation (with consent and anonymized
data) and compare the product output against the mess's accepted result.

## 6. Risks

-   Accounting practices vary significantly by mess.
-   Members may resist daily data entry.
-   Managers may not want to expose all financial details.
-   Free spreadsheets may be "good enough" for very small groups.

Mitigate with configurable policies, simple daily entry, clear
permissions and transparent statements.

## 7. Possible pricing (validate before launch)

-   Free pilot for one mess and a limited member count.
-   Low-cost monthly plan for larger messes or multiple managers.
-   Optional paid exports/advanced reminders only if users value them.

Do not build payment subscriptions before validating willingness to pay.
